# About this app

Manage your PG, hostel, and rental properties with ease using ZUZUStay. Track rooms, tenants, monthly rent status, occupancy, and property performance—all from one simple and secure app.

ZUZUStay helps property owners and managers save time by digitizing daily operations, reducing paperwork, and keeping important information organized.

### 🏠 Property Management

Manage one or multiple PGs, hostels, or rental properties from a single account.

### 🛏 Room & Bed Management

Create rooms, assign beds, track occupancy, and view available vacancies instantly.

### 👥 Tenant Management

Store tenant details, contact information, ID information, check-in details, and room assignments securely.

### 💰 Monthly Rent Management

Track monthly rent for every tenant and easily mark payments as **Paid** or **Pending** to know who has completed their rent and who still needs to pay.

### 📊 Dashboard & Insights

Get a quick overview of:

* Total Properties
* Total Tenants
* Occupancy Percentage
* Available Beds
* Monthly Occupancy Chart

### 📱 Easy Property Management

Manage your properties anytime, anywhere using a simple and user-friendly interface designed for busy property managers.

### 🔒 Secure Data Storage

Your property and tenant information is stored securely to help keep your business data safe.

### ⚡ Fast & Simple

Designed for speed and ease of use so you can complete daily property management tasks in just a few taps.

### 📈 Built for Growing Businesses

Whether you manage a single PG or multiple properties, ZUZUStay helps you organize your operations efficiently as your business grows.

### ⭐ Key Features

✅ Manage multiple properties

✅ Add and organize rooms & beds

✅ Create and manage tenant profiles

✅ Track room occupancy and vacancies

✅ Mark monthly rent as Paid or Pending

✅ Property dashboard with occupancy insights

✅ Simple and easy-to-use interface

✅ Secure cloud-based data storage

✅ Regular updates with new features

ZUZUStay is the perfect property management solution for PG owners, hostel operators, co-living spaces, and rental property managers looking to simplify daily operations and manage everything from one place.
